Intercropping in ETH LSMS - ISA

Below I explore cereal legume intercropping in Ethiopia employing LSMS-ISA data. The employed data is from wave 4 which has been collected in 2018-2019.

We should not observe any pure stand in the above tibble. However, this happens because in some cases the same field of the parcel is listed for e.g. maize as a pure stand while there is also a mix of maize and legume on the same field. Thus, the total count below reflects the true distribution of intercropping. This is likely an enumerator error.
